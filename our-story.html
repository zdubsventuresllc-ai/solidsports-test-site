Our Story

The Origin

A father and son who grew up on softball diamonds together.

Solid didn't start in a boardroom. It started in the dugout — decades of early mornings, late-night batting practice, rec league games where the only thing that mattered was the next at-bat. Softball has always been the thing that kept us together.

After all those years of playing, we saw the same thing over and over: great bats that stood for nothing, and causes that needed a platform. So we built one.

Why We Exist

62% of kids are priced out of youth sports. The bat industry spends millions on marketing but nothing on the communities that make the sport possible. We started Solid to change that.

The Partner

Pure Sports Technologies.
Our Manufacturing Partner.

Pure Sports Technologies is the only US-based composite bat manufacturer. They build the best bats in the country — no shortcuts, no overseas sourcing. Made in USA by people who know bats.

When we set out to build Solid, there was only one call to make. We partnered with Pure Sports because their standards match ours. That partnership is the foundation everything else is built on.
