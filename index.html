Our Story

Built by a father and son who believe the game deserves better.

Solid started with a simple truth: the best bats should do more than perform. They should stand for something. We grew up in the game together — countless hours of practice, early mornings, late nights, years of competing side by side.

We partnered with Pure Sports Technologies — the only US-based composite bat manufacturer — because we believe in making the best product possible, right here at home.
